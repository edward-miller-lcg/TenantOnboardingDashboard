Location.type Mapping

NHSNLink uses HSLOC (Healthcare Service Location Code) codes to categorize patient locations. To ensure accurate reporting, you must map your facility's location codes to the NHSN-standard HSLOC codes within the NHSN application before proceeding.

Navigate to the NHSN application and complete the HSLOC code mapping under your facility settings. Once complete, check the box below to confirm.
